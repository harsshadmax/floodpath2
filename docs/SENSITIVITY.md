## Reading the sweep
- **Moderate is fairly stable**: reach-any and coverage move by only a few points across all five settings; the multiplier mainly shifts the mean extra time (5.3 to 6.8 min).
- **Severe is not stable at the cautious end**: tightening the thresholds to 0.05/0.35 collapses reach-any to 0% (every facility becomes unreachable), matching the "the network collapses between 25% and 30% of reports" finding in PHASE 10/11. The headline severe numbers (75% reach, 53.5% coverage) hold only away from that edge; a plausible, slightly more cautious threshold erases them entirely.
- **Conclusion for the demo**: present moderate's numbers as reasonably robust to the threshold choice; present severe's numbers as describing one side of a cliff, not a stable estimate, and say so explicitly rather than implying precision the model doesn't have.
